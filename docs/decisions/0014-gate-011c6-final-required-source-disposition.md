## Decision

`ACCEPTED_BLOCKED` means that the Source cannot produce healthy, complete
FULL_SOURCE truth under the current scientific, access and acquisition
contracts. It is not a claim of permanent impossibility.

The primary audit classes are governance documentation, not database states:

- `POLICY_BLOCKED`
- `TECHNICAL_RELIABILITY_BLOCKED`
- `SEMANTIC_IDENTITY_BLOCKED`
- `SOURCE_UNIVERSE_BLOCKED`
- `MULTI_SURFACE_BLOCKED`

No robots, authentication, browser-acquisition, Vacancy, Source, geography,
dedup, green, threshold or freshness contract is weakened. Production remains
governed GET/POST. A working subset never establishes FULL_SOURCE.
